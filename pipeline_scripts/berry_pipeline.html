Four commands and it's posted

Everything in the folder hangs off one straight line: refresh, check, look, post. Everything else is a branch you only take when something is wrong. Tap any step to open it.

  1. 1
    python3 refresh.py

    Takes a few minutes. The slow part is the scrapers.

    1. 1ig_sync.pyoptional Asks Instagram what is already up, so anything you posted from your phone gets filed and its listings never come back on a future slide.
    2. 2seek.py Scrape Seek.
    3. 3LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Google The other boards.
    4. 4check_live.pyoptional Asks each board whether a listing actually closed, rather than guessing from it being absent.
    5. 5build_site.py Rebuilds berry_internships.html on your disk.
    6. 6deploy_site.pyoptional Puts that file live on internberry.netlify.app. No token in .env means it tells you how to set one up and the run carries on.
    7. 7build_carousels.py Renders every slide, with --rebuild so a style change actually reaches the queue.

    If a step fails. The three marked optional cannot stop the run. A missing Netlify token should never cost you your carousels.

    python3 refresh.py --fast

    Skips the slow scrapers when you only want a rebuild.

  2. 2
    python3 dashboard.py

    The schedule. Monday is Technology, Data & AI. Thursday is Business, Commerce, Marketing & Finance. Saturday is Engineering. When a category has more roles than fit in one post, the overflow lands on the next day.

    The dashboard also carries the logo queue and a line saying what is actually on Instagram, which goes amber when that snapshot is more than a day old.

  3. 3
    python3 preview.py

    Nothing is uploaded and nothing is changed. It reads the JPEGs straight off disk, so what you see is the actual file that would go out, not a mock-up of it.

    Worth doing. This is where you catch a duplicate listing, a missing logo or a title that got truncated, while it still costs nothing to fix.

  4. 4
    python3 publish_to_instagram.py

    Posts whatever is due today.

    python3 publish_to_instagram.py --dry-run

    Walks the whole thing and publishes nothing.

    python3 publish_to_instagram.py technology-data-ai-part1

    One named carousel instead of whatever is due.

    Why it takes a minute. Instagram never accepts a file. The slides are pushed to a public GitHub URL first, then Meta fetches them itself, then the post is published. Three steps, and the middle one is out of your hands.

    Once it succeeds the carousel moves out of the queue and its listings are recorded, so the same six jobs can never appear on a future slide.

When something is off

Each of these is a detour off the line above. You take one, then go back to where you were.

Nothing here guesses. A wrong logo is worse than an initials tile: a tile reads as a small business, the wrong mark reads as someone who does not check.

python3 missing_logos.py

Who is missing one, most common first.

python3 find_logos.py

Goes and finds candidate domains, then puts the uncertain ones in the dashboard for you to answer.

python3 find_logos.py --accept "Arup"

Yes, that candidate is them.

python3 find_logos.py --set "ADP Consulting" adpconsulting.com

No, it is this one instead. Use this when the finder's candidate is a real site belonging to somebody else.

python3 build_carousels.py --rebuild

Then rebuild, or the slides keep the tile.

Building and deploying are separate on purpose, so you can look at the file before the world does. If the live site disagrees with your disk, the deploy is what's missing.

python3 deploy_site.py --check

What is live against what is on your disk.

python3 deploy_site.py

Push it.

Then hard refresh. Cmd+Shift+R. A normal refresh serves you the cached old file and you will think nothing happened.

Every other script assumes posts only reach Instagram through the publisher. Post from your phone and the carousel stays in the queue and its listings are never marked as used, so the same six jobs come back on a future slide.

python3 ig_sync.py --list

What is actually on the account.

python3 ig_sync.py

Files it properly: history, used listings, queue. It only ever adds, never deletes.

It matches posts to carousels by the job links in the caption, not the caption text, because links are the one part nobody rewrites.

python3 more_backgrounds.py

Pulls fresh bright photos into background pics/.

python3 more_backgrounds.py "sunlit cafe laptop"

Search for something specific. Bright, one subject, plenty of empty surface, because the white card covers the middle.

python3 build_carousels.py --rebuild

Rebuild so the new photos get used.

Unsplash only. Photos off Google Images are not safe to post on a monetised account.

python3 publish_reel.py path/to/video.mp4

Put the caption in a .txt file of the same name next to the video. Typing a caption into the terminal mangles emoji and eats quote marks, which is a miserable way to find out your caption posted wrong.

Slower than slides. Meta has to download and transcode the video before it can publish, which is a minute or two rather than a few seconds.

python3 commands.py

Every command in the folder, grouped. Also prints at the bottom of every refresh and dashboard run.

python3 commands.py logo

Only the ones mentioning a word.

Every command runs from the same folder. The name ends in a real space, so it has to be quoted or the shell splits it in two.
